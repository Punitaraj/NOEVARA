Case Files

A note on these: the case files below are drawn from real NOEVARA engagements. Identifying details - company name, exact figures, and anything else that could reveal a client's identity - have been removed or generalised to protect confidentiality. The diagnostic tools used, the findings, and the shape of each engagement are accurate to the work itself.
Case File · 01

Mid-Sized Industrial Manufacturer

Profile
~220 employees, multi-site, France
Presenting Symptoms
Missed delivery targets, slow cross-department decisions, rising early-career attrition - with no consensus internally on the actual cause.
Diagnostic Used
OrgScan
Findings
Root cause traced to a leadership communication breakdown between plant operations and commercial teams - not, as first assumed, a staffing shortage.
Treatment Plan
90-day roadmap plus embedded Transformation Execution Office support to rebuild the operations–commercial handoff.
Outcome (Generalised)
Decision cycles for cross-department issues shortened noticeably within the first quarter of the intervention.
Case File · 02

Regional Financial Services Firm

Profile
~90 employees, regulated sector
Presenting Symptoms
Three AI pilots launched over 18 months, none scaled past a proof of concept - leadership had lost confidence in further AI investment.
Diagnostic Used
AI Maturity Lens
Findings
Maturity gaps in data governance and leadership readiness - not the AI tools themselves - were the recurring reason pilots stalled.
Treatment Plan
Phased roadmap addressing governance gaps first, with a Fractional CAIO engagement to own portfolio prioritisation going forward.
Outcome (Generalised)
First AI initiative to scale past pilot stage since the programme began was delivered within two quarters.
Case File · 03

Public-Sector-Adjacent Services Body

Profile
~150 staff, multiple external data-sharing agreements
Presenting Symptoms
Uncertainty over AI Act and GDPR exposure ahead of a planned monitoring-tool rollout, with no internal legal capacity to assess it quickly.
Diagnostic Used
EU Regulatory Radar EUTIS
Findings
The planned tool triggered high-risk obligations under the AI Act that hadn't been identified internally, alongside two lower-priority GDPR gaps.
Treatment Plan
Scoped remediation proposal generated same-day; governance framework and evidence pack built ahead of rollout, not after.
Outcome (Generalised)
Rollout proceeded on the original timeline with documented conformity in place before go-live.
