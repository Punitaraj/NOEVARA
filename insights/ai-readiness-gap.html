The AI Readiness Gap: What 40 SME Assessments Actually Found

Where readiness really breaks down across strategy, data, governance, talent and value — and why it's rarely the technology.

AI adoption is often presented as a technology problem. Our assessment work with small and medium-sized enterprises tells a different story.

Across 40 SME assessments, the largest barriers were frequently found outside the technology itself — in strategy, organizational readiness, data quality, governance and the ability to translate AI investments into measurable business value.

This whitepaper examines the recurring patterns and practical lessons that emerged from those assessments.
