Applied Engineering & Technical Consulting

Rigorous, independent engineering analysis for specific technical questions — feasibility studies, process optimization, industrial problem-solving.

Overview

When a technical proposal is written by the same team that will deliver it, leadership is being asked to trust the mark on its own homework. We provide an independent, evidence-based second opinion — a documented root cause, a feasibility matrix, and a recommendation your board can interrogate.
